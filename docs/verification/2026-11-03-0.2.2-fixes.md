# BVD 0.2.2 - the three reported issues: fix and in-game evidence (2026-11-03)

Build 42.21.0, ZombieBuddy layer, Java version 3.7, mod version 0.2.2 (commit c3740cd plus test-only edits listed
at the end). Singleplayer started with `-debug`; dedicated server with two clients. Logs and screenshots are in
`evidence-2026-11-03/`. Not uploaded: this is a release candidate.

## 1. "Modded cars feel extremely fast after the update" (reference power & weight on)

**What I found**
- A car's power is stored per car when it spawns (script force x a quality factor) and saved with the world. Cars that
  spawned while 0.1.19-0.2.0 wrote the 4x boost into the vehicle scripts already carry it. 0.2.1 moved the boost into
  the Java and applied it to the stored power, so those cars got it twice (up to the 28000 cap).
- Separately, the GripLevel slider is applied twice in the launch force (once in the grip factor, once in the traction
  limit), so a setting of 1.5 acts like 2.25. The Casual preset uses 1.5.

**What I changed**
- A car whose stored power is already above 1.05x its script's own force is not boosted again.
- New option `AccelCeiling` (Drivetrain, default 4, 0 = off): the drive force can never exceed mass x this, so it
  holds the same for every car, vanilla or modded, whatever its engine, tires, mods or sliders.

**Evidence**
- `power_test_log.txt` (mode `power`, 7/7): option off uses the stored power as it is (4000); option on gives a normal
  car 16000, a worn engine (70%) 11200, and a car that already carries the old boost stays 16000. With the guard
  switched off on the same build (the 0.2.1 behaviour) that same car is boosted again: power used 28000. That is the
  bug, and the fix, side by side.
- `accel_sweep_log.txt` (mode `accel_sweep`, median of 3 runs per case, drive limit read from the controller):

  | setting | drive limit per kg | 0-50 km/h | speed at 3 s |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | default sliders, ceiling 4 | 2.09 | 3.7 - 7.5 s (slow runs are the harness's stuck-spawn quirk) | 16 km/h |
  | Casual-like (power 1.4, grip 1.5), no ceiling | 4.71 | 2.6 s | 60 km/h |
  | Casual-like, ceiling 4 | 4.00 | 3.0 s | 50 km/h |
  | grip 2.0 (slider max), no ceiling | 10.00 | 2.2 s | 63 km/h |
  | grip 2.0, ceiling 4 | 4.00 | 3.2 s | 45 km/h |
  | power 5 + grip 2.0, ceiling 4 | 4.00 | 3.1 s | 47 km/h |

- The launch limit scales with GripLevel squared: 2.09 per kg at 1.0, 4.71 at 1.5 (x2.25), 10.00 at 2.0. That is the
  grip slider being applied twice, measured in game.
- `accel_extreme_log.txt` (mode `accel_extreme`, median of 3 runs): the most extreme launch the settings and a modded
  tire profile allow (race tires road grip 3.0, GripLevel 2.0, power 5): drive limit 30.00 per kg without the ceiling,
  4.00 with it; 0-50 km/h in 2.2 s versus 2.5 s, 63 versus 60 km/h at 3 s. Default sliders: 2.09 per kg, 0-50 in 3.8 s.
- The info panel now also shows "Engine force" and "Launch limit", so a reporter can read the real numbers.

**What I could not show:** the report said 120 km/h in about 3 seconds. The most I measured with every lever pushed
(slider maxima, a race-tire profile, the ceiling off) was 63-80 km/h at 3 s, because from that point power and
gearing, not traction, set the pace. So the double boost and the double grip are real and fixed or bounded, but the
literal figure is not reproducible here. It needs the reporter's sandbox settings, car and other mods; the new panel
rows will show the numbers.

## 2. "With CleanUI installed the BVD menu inside the mechanics window does not work"

**What I found:** I could not reproduce it. With CleanUI [B42] (Workshop 3437629766) and NeatUI Framework (3508537032)
on 42.21.0, BVD's panel shows beside the Vehicle Mechanics window with all nine rows. Neither mod touches
`ISVehicleMechanics` (searched both, plus the Mickey, JKB and Compat forks, which only target 42.20.4).

**Evidence:** `mechanics_with_cleanui.png` (CleanUI's bars are visible at the top, the BVD panel is right of the
window) and `mechanics_plain.png` for comparison; `mechanics_with_cleanui_log.txt` and `mechanics_plain_log.txt`
(mode `mechanics` / `mechanics_cleanui`, 8/8 each): window opens, panel created, visible, 9 rows, beside the window.

**The failure I could reproduce, and its fix, in game:** the panel used to hide itself when neither side of the window
had room (a small game window, a big UI scale, a wide window). With CleanUI + NeatUI loaded at 1024x768
(`mechanics_narrow_log.txt`, mode `mechanics_narrow`, 8/8): the placement used before 0.2.2 leaves the panel hidden
(`visible=false`, `mechanics_narrow_before.png`: no panel); the current placement shows it fully on screen with its
rows (`mechanics_narrow_after.png`; it sits over the right edge of the window because nothing else fits, and the console
carries the one-line "[BVD] info panel: no free spot" note). It now tries right, left, below, above, then pins to the
screen edge. `panel_dock_unit_test_output.txt`: 7/7, including thousands of screen and window sizes.

**Still needed from the reporter** if it still fails: screen size, UI scale, which UI mods, and the CleanUI version.

## 3. "Tire marks start well behind the car"

**What I found:** each wheel kept its last mark and, at the next brake, drift or launch, drew a line from that old
spot to the car (up to 20 tiles), and took the first heading from it too.

**What I changed:** a mark only joins the streak if the previous one is under 0.3 s old.

**Evidence** (`tire_marks_log.txt`, mode `skidstreak`, 6/6): two launches 12-16 tiles apart, the second run with the
old behaviour forced and the second as shipped:
- old behaviour: longest fill from the earlier skid 13.7 tiles, no fresh streak (the bug, reproduced);
- as shipped: 2 fresh streaks (one per rear wheel), longest fill 0.0 tiles, first mark 1.3 tiles from the car.
`tire_marks_rule_unit_test_output.txt`: the streak rule, 6/6 offline.
